Welcome to JC Cabs Ooty – Driving Smiles Across the Nilgiris

Founded in the heart of Ooty Town on Commercial Road, JC Cabs was created with a simple vision: to offer travelers clean, reliable, and stress-free taxi services across Ooty, Coonoor, Kotagiri, and outstation routes.

Navigating Nilgiri mountain roads with 36 steep hairpin bends requires skill, patience, and deep local knowledge. All our drivers are seasoned Nilgiri locals who prioritize passenger safety, punctuality, and courteous service.

Whether you need a quick airport transfer from Coimbatore (CJB), a 1-day local sightseeing tour, or a multi-day family holiday package, JC Cabs ensures flat transparent pricing with zero surprise charges.
